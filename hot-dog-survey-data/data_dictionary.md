Data Dictionary for Hot Dog Survey Dataset

Dataset Overview: 
This dataset contains survey responses regarding our essential research question: Is A Hot Dog a Sandwich? 
The data was collected through an online survey conducted in 2025. 
 
| Column Name | Description | Data Type | Example Value | 
|-------------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Timestamp | Marks the creation of a record with a timestamp. | object | 1/14/2026 21:53:05 |
| Year at UVA |What current year of study are you in| object | 1st Year,Graduate student,etc |
| What is your Gender Identity|Inquires on respondents' gender | object |"Male"|
| What school are you part of|Inquires on respondents' department of study | object |"Data science, Arts and Science"|
| Is hotdog a sandwhich|Inquires on respondents' rationale on the inquiring claim | object |"No"|
| How Strongly do you feel about it|Follow up on respondant's previous question| object |"5"|
| How frequently do you eat hotdog|Inquires on respondents' eating behavior | object |"Frequent"|
